New in 1.9

Facets and labels

A collection can be viewed from different sides: by category or folder, by author, by series, by what you opened lately. There are ten facets in all: see where the view is best.

Want more labels? Tags, a rating (its tab is switched on in Settings, like all the others) and colours: a book can have several of them now. Thanks to Victoria for the idea.

Complex conditions? Easy!

Red and blue. Not green. Favourites only. Science fiction and mysteries. No short stories. Rated three and higher. Added in the last week. In ContinuousReader also finished from January to March. A query you made stays as a line in the sections’ column.

ContinuousReader in the system’s own look

Do you like an app to look like part of the system? Switch on the library’s system interface in Settings: a split view, the tabs as rows in the sidebar, an inspector. On the Mac and the iPad.

A filter by reading — not started, reading or finished — works wherever you are: in a folder, at an author, among the tags. The New tab is no longer needed and is gone.
