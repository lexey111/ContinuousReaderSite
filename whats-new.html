1.9 “Painted Lady”

“Show your colours” — colours, tags and a rating, and seven more ways to look at a collection

November 2026. Three books in JustReader, a completely new PlainReader and two new readers in the family. Below are the main things, with pictures; every change, item by item, is in the changelog.

Earlier releases Highlights of 1.8 “Map” Highlights of 1.7 “Orient Express” Highlights of 1.6 “Make yourself comfortable” Highlights of 1.5 “I like to move it”

Three books in JustReader

JustReader keeps three active books now. They show as cards or as a grid, side by side or one under another, depending on the device; on the iPhone they are pages you swipe between.

Paste an address, and JustReader fetches the book and opens it. The history remembers ten books now, and downloads a fetched one again when needed.

A completely new PlainReader

The shelf stands in one, two or three columns: sections, books and the book itself. When the window narrows, the extra columns go, and later they come back. The info panel can stay beside the book. On the iPhone the shelf opens step by step.

Facets and labels

A collection can be viewed from different sides: by category or folder, by author, by series, by what you opened lately. There are ten facets in all: see where the view is best.

Want more labels? Tags, a rating (its tab is switched on in Settings, like all the others) and colours: a book can have several of them now. Thanks to Victoria for the idea.

Complex conditions? Easy!

Red and blue. Not green. Favourites only. Science fiction and mysteries. No short stories. Rated three and higher. Added in the last week. In ContinuousReader also finished from January to March. A query you made stays as a line in the sections’ column.

ContinuousReader in the system’s own look

Do you like an app to look like part of the system? Switch on the library’s system interface in Settings: a split view, the tabs as rows in the sidebar, an inspector. On the Mac and the iPad.

A filter by reading — not started, reading or finished — works wherever you are: in a folder, at an author, among the tags. The New tab is no longer needed and is gone.

Borders, and Comfort

A patterned strip above the text and under it: vine, scrolls, lozenges, braid, meander, guilloche or laurel. Comfort has paper textures now and reads right to left.

BookCommander

The Terminal skin is called BookCommander now, and PlainReader has it too. If you have a keyboard, there is a small Easter egg in it: click the command line and type help.

The family has grown!

WebReader, which opens in a browser, and NanoReader for Windows, Linux and Mac have joined the three apps for Apple devices. The map of all the readers is in About and on the family’s page.
