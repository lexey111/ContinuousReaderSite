Five readers, one reading engine

A nightstand, a shelf, a library.

One reader, different furniture around it.

Reading a book or two and downloading the next when you finish? JustReader. Keeping a shelf a month ahead and finding the next book in an OPDS catalogue? PlainReader. Hundreds or thousands of books that you read on several devices? ContinuousReader. Not on Apple’s devices? NanoReader for Windows, Linux and Mac, or WebReader in any browser.

The family

One reading engine: in a browser, on Apple’s devices and on other computers.

Web Apple Windows · Linux · Mac

WebReader

In a browser · Free

The reader in a browser tab, simpler than in the apps. Works on any device, with nothing to install. The book is stored and processed locally, on the device you opened it on.

Three books is a deliberate limit: these are books without a library. For a library there are PlainReader (the advanced level) and ContinuousReader (the professional one).

PlainReader

The bookshelf · One-time purchase · iPhone, iPad, Mac

For reading several books at once, or keeping them on a shelf to come back to later. Every book keeps its bookmarks and notes. Not a library like ContinuousReader, but a really long shelf, with coloured sections and filters.

One reading engine for all. Bought once for Mac, iPad and iPhone, no subscription, with Family Sharing.

App StoreAbout it

ContinuousReader

The library · One-time purchase · iPhone, iPad, Mac

A library for thousands of books: folders inside folders, tags, authors and series, search through the text of every book. OPDS catalogs watch for new arrivals. And the cherry on top: the library syncs between devices, and your place in the book in real time.

One reading engine for all. Bought once for Mac, iPad and iPhone, no subscription, with Family Sharing.

In the three apps for Apple

The same reading.

The reader you spend your evening in is the same one in all three. Wherever you start, and wherever you move later, there is nothing to get used to again.

  • The pageScroll or pages, up to four columns, real typography with hyphenation and justification, paper, frames and borders. And two readers to choose from, Comfort and Professional.
  • The lookForty-two themes, day and night, including two that dress the whole page — Typewriter, and BookCommander in its Norton frame.
  • The wordsTranslation of a selected fragment, Read Aloud with each word lit as it goes, and search inside the book
  • What the book keepsBookmarks with colours, notes, the place you stopped and every sitting in the reading journal. They belong to the book, not to its copy on the disk. Statistics, the journal and the notes have windows of their own.
  • Every deviceNative on Mac, iPad and iPhone, bought once for all three. No subscriptions anywhere in the family.

Changing apps means moving your books, nothing more. There is no upgrade between them: each is bought on its own, and once. Your reading settings move whole, in one .crsettings file.
