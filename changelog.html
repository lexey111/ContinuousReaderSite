Release history

What’s New

Every ContinuousReader release, newest first. JustReader shares the reading engine, so engine changes apply to both apps.

1.3 “Swallowtail” — July 2026

  • Folders show up right among the books — first in the list and in the card grid, with their own colors and book counts; move a book into a folder by simply dragging it.
  • The iPhone library is rebuilt: reworked rows, separate buttons for folders, sorting, and filters, finger-friendly sheets.
  • iPad catches up with the Mac: a full settings screen, a status bar with the active view’s controls, adjustable sidebar folder size.
  • Views fit the screen: smooth card sizing, covers at every list detail level, control bars that adapt to the window width.
  • The whole app follows the hue of your reading theme — library and reader alike, on Mac, iPad, and iPhone.
  • A more responsive library: covers load in the background at the displayed size, large grids scroll without stutters, Mac context menus no longer close on their own.
  • JustReader: a new home screen — a round Read button wrapped in a progress gauge — plus a history of the last five books, each reopening on its page.
  • Fixes and polish throughout.

1.2 “Morpho” — July 2026

  • Margins mode comes to iPad: the page floats as a sheet on a themed desk, with its own settings dialog and a fullscreen quick-menu entry.
  • Page turns on the margins: the desk around the page turns pages — forward on the right and bottom, back on the left and top.
  • Translation settings gain a language-pair picker; the selection menu leads there when the pair isn’t set.
  • Mark as Read clears the NEW badge without opening the book — for one book, a selection, or a whole folder.
  • Folder tree and per-folder counts on the New tab; subtler New markers on iPhone.
  • Fixes and polish throughout.

1.1 “Apollo” — July 2026

  • OPDS Watchers dashboard — watch catalog feeds, check in the background, import new arrivals in one click, with an import log.
  • New badges and a New library tab collect books you haven’t started, with per-folder counts.
  • Margins mode on Mac: the page floats as a sheet on a themed desk — wood, leather, blueprint grids or your own wallpaper.
  • Illustrations match the theme: monochrome engravings blend into the page color, transparent line art stays transparent.
  • Cleaner card view: more air, a cover-only mode and reading progress on the card.
  • Native multi-select on Mac (⌘-click, ⇧-click, rubber-band) — dragging moves the whole selection.
  • Continue Reading: reopen your last book with one shortcut or pick up where another device left off.
  • Steadier sync: no more position rewinds after sleep; the Latest tab catches up in seconds.
  • A What’s New screen in the app, plus many fixes and polish.

1.0.1 — July 8, 2026

  • Rock-solid two-page reading on iPad — stable across app switching and rotation.
  • Battery indicator and configurable status sections in fullscreen.
  • Fixed keyboard scrolling jitter with external keyboards.
  • Safer book imports and polish throughout.

1.0 “Monarch” — July 7, 2026

  • First public release: the library with folders and cards, the continuous reader with 41 themes, iCloud sync, OPDS catalogs, translation, Read Aloud and reading statistics — on Mac, iPad and iPhone.
